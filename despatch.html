Two minutes on the radio

You are the despatcher for Brighton city centre. Eighteen calls will come in and you have seven units. You cannot cover everything, so read each call, judge the risk and send the right resource.

  • Pick a call in the queue or on the map, then pick a unit (or press 1 to 7). Send a second unit as backup the same way.
  • The grade comes from the call taker. Read the text anyway: a few calls are graded wrongly, and the wording tells you which unit is needed.
  • Units stay committed at scene, so low-risk jobs can wait. Never leave an emergency waiting to service a nuisance call.
